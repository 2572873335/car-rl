========================================================================================
IS _obs() SAFE TO CALL AS A SIDE PROBE?  (it reads self.prev_e)
========================================================================================

  PLAIN env (correct usage: obs from step() return)
    reset: obs[1] = +0.000000
    step 0: obs[1] = +0.000000   (prev_e now +0.410114)
    step 1: obs[1] = -0.056290   (prev_e now +0.407863)
    step 2: obs[1] = +0.080000   (prev_e now +0.411063)
    step 3: obs[1] = -0.023720   (prev_e now +0.410114)

  SIDE-PROBE usage: _obs() called separately before step()
    reset: _obs()[1] = +0.000000
    step 0: side-probe _obs()[1] = +0.000000   step-return obs[1] = +0.000000
    step 1: side-probe _obs()[1] = +0.000000   step-return obs[1] = -0.056290
    step 2: side-probe _obs()[1] = +0.000000   step-return obs[1] = +0.080000
    step 3: side-probe _obs()[1] = +0.000000   step-return obs[1] = -0.023720

  => if the side-probe column is not equal to the step-return column,
     then calling _obs() as a probe gives a DIFFERENT (stale) value,
     and AttackerEnv.step() -- which does exactly that -- feeds the
     defender a wrong obs[1].
