D0 plan v2: revised after review1 R1-R5 (5 blocking items, all reproduced)

Review1 rejected the D0 plan with 5 blocking items. I independently verified
the load-bearing ones before revising:

R1 (confirmed): the "stage A measurement-only, don't touch D_DES" design is
void. follow_env computes e = gap - D_DES with D_DES always 0.20, so claiming
to measure 0.50/1.00 gap by only changing the reporting formula produces a
constant 30/80 cm "error" that says nothing about the policy (RL 30.01 vs
P+FF 29.89 -- indistinguishable).

R2 (confirmed, and it reverses the cost calculus): the CORRECT measurement
requires changing D_DES, and once you do, the frozen checkpoint works
zero-shot (d=0.50 -> 0.40 true-cm, d=1.00 -> 0.45 true-cm, zero collision).
The V2V speed prior + relative-error observation make the gap axis generalize
structurally other than by learning. So "retraining" on the gap axis is
provably unnecessary; the expensive thing the plan designed around does not
exist.

R4 (confirmed, the most valuable finding): the headline "RL 2x better than
hand-tuned" REVERSES on the AGV-relevant grid. At d=0.50 v=1.0 m/s, RL
(5.63 true-cm) loses to P+FF (5.09 true-cm). Mechanism: slew-rate saturation
of a_max at high speed; PPO only learned low-speed small residuals, while
P+FF's direct v_l + kp*e large-step correction holds up. The plan did not
predict this nor list it in the risk table. The audit exists precisely to
expose this truth, and it must be reported honestly, with the README headline
qualified to the training distribution (<= 0.5 m/s).

Also corrected per the review: braking distance formula is right (velocity
channel is slew-limited, no tau); the 1.0x0.20 cell is NOT physically doomed
(zero-collision at constant speed; RL crashes 6/10 only when the leader
brakes); and D_DES does NOT enter termination (that uses absolute gap
0.12/2.0).

v2 plan changes:
  - stage A/B split replaced by "change D_DES + re-evaluate only, no retrain"
  - unit convention declared (obs-cm = 200*|e|, the repo convention)
  - thresholds fixed (v1's 12.5% was miscalculated; v1's 5cm would fail the
    measured 1.0 m/s value)
  - in-distribution / out-of-distribution boundary defined a priori, with
    positive-evidence requirements so the audit cannot become "cannot fail"
  - the v=1.0 reversal reported as a finding, not hidden

Author self-check artifact D0_stageA_selfcheck.md is retained; its load-bearing
claim (runtime D_DES override works) is consistent with R2's fix, but its
proposal to shrink the matrix to spacing-only is superseded by the review's
decision to keep all 9 cells.

Frozen files NOT yet modified. The D_DES change (iron rule 2) is the first
execution step and awaits review2 approval.
